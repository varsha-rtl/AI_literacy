2. **Which two concepts are easy to confuse?**  
   Learning paradigms and task types are easy to confuse. A learning paradigm describes the kind of signal or information used for learning, such as labels, structure in data, or rewards. A task type describes the output needed, such as a class, number, group, anomaly indication, or ranking. For example, classification is often supervised, while anomaly detection can be approached with or without labelled examples.
